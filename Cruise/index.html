Cruise FAQ

Common cruise passenger questions.

Can I book if I arrive by cruise ship?

Yes. These excursions are made for cruise passengers arriving in Roatan with limited port time.

Do you pick up from the cruise port?

Yes. We can arrange pickup from Coxen Hole / Port of Roatan and Mahogany Bay / Isla Tropicale area.

Will I get back to the ship on time?

The trip is planned around your ship schedule with a comfortable return-to-port buffer before departure.

Do I need fishing experience?

No. We can build beginner-friendly trips, serious angler trips, or family-friendly mixed experiences.

Is gear included?

Yes. Gear can be included so you do not need to bring fishing equipment from the ship.

What happens if my ship arrives late?

Message us on WhatsApp. When possible, we adjust the plan to the updated arrival time and remaining port window.

Can my family come if they do not fish?

Yes. Mixed groups can add light fishing, sightseeing, a beach stop, or snorkeling depending on schedule and conditions.
